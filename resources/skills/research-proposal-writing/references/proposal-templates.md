# Route-specific proposal architecture

Use the supplied institution or call document first. These are defaults when no template is supplied.

| Route | Lead with | Required emphasis | Common omission to check |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Grant | Problem, significance, actionable aims | Feasibility, team/resources, milestones, budget logic, risk and alternative | Aims too broad for the available time and funds |
| Thesis/dissertation | Research gap and theoretical contribution | Research questions, literature position, methods, ethics, chapter/timeline plan | Methods cannot answer the stated question |
| Fellowship | Candidate and training trajectory tied to the study | Mentorship, skill acquisition, independence, project feasibility | Research plan and development plan are disconnected |
| Course | Focused question and achievable method | Clear scope, rubric alignment, data access, learning goal | Grand claims beyond a short assignment |
| Internal | Decision to be made and evidence needed | Operational constraints, decision threshold, governance | Deliverables lack a decision owner |

Preserve word/page limits. Do not invent a sponsor-specific section, required declaration, or review criterion. A call for proposals may require a different order or additional material.
