# Statistical choices and interpretation

The core engine minimizes the restricted likelihood for between-study τ², then estimates the inverse-variance weighted mean under a random-effects model. The summary standard error uses the Hartung–Knapp adjustment with an ad-hoc floor preventing it from being smaller than the conventional Wald SE; the CI uses a t quantile with k−1 degrees of freedom. This is a defensible default, not a universal claim that the interval is always conservative. With very few studies, any heterogeneity estimate is uncertain. The prediction interval uses τ² plus adjusted summary uncertainty and a t quantile with k−2 degrees of freedom; interpret it as a model-based range for a similar future setting, not an assurance about every individual study.

For OR, RR and HR, the script converts reported ratios and CI limits to natural logs, derives SE from the log CI, fits on that scale, and exponentiates the pooled estimates and limits. SMD is assumed to be a properly computed standardized mean difference supplied by the source; the engine does not derive Hedges' correction from arm-level SDs. It does not pool one risk ratio with a hazard ratio or mix RCT and observational designs without a separate prespecified analysis.

The engine produces source-level forest and descriptive funnel plots, leave-one-out pooled estimates, and a compact heterogeneity panel. Its `standardized_shift` is a descriptive leave-one-out shift in units of the full model's summary SE. The `influence` array also reports a one-parameter weighted-least-squares leverage, internally studentized residual and Cook's D **conditional on the full-fit REML τ² held fixed**. Thus these diagnostics are screening aids, not an exact re-estimated-τ² influence analysis. Investigate extreme studies for extraction errors and substantive differences, then document decisions transparently. Egger regression is skipped for k<10 and for ratio measures; even when run, it tests small-study effects, not publication bias itself. See the [current Cochrane Handbook, chapter 10](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-10), [chapter 13](https://www.cochrane.org/authors/handbooks-and-manuals/handbook/current/chapter-13), and [metafor model documentation](https://wviechtb.github.io/metafor/reference/rma.uni.html) for method context and caveats.
