# English and Chinese academic prose

Use the requested manuscript language while keeping progress messages in the user's language. A bilingual brief is not a request for a bilingual final article. Keep a small terminology record when translations affect the argument: original term, chosen rendering, source edition, and a relevant rejected alternative.

## Shared standards

Use concrete subjects and exact verbs. State an established point directly; attach uncertainty to the specific inference that needs it. Preserve scope, negation, causal force and attribution when translating. Distinguish association, interpretation and causation. A sophisticated argument can be expressed plainly.

Every paragraph should add an analytical step, not paraphrase its predecessor. Transitions explain a relationship (contrast, qualification, implication), rather than a sequence of generic signposts. Read for unintended contradictions and grammatical reversals. Check that pronouns and abstract nouns refer to something identifiable. Do not inflate a sentence to make it sound scholarly.

## English

Use stable terms for the same concept. Prefer precise qualifications (“in these accounts”, “may help explain”) to repeated blanket hedges. Avoid noun piles, unsupported superlatives and rhetorical claims that a topic is universally important. Do not copy a source's syntax while swapping synonyms. Retain disciplinary first person when appropriate; passive voice is a choice, not proof of objectivity.

## 中文

先明确“谁、依据什么材料、作出了什么判断”，再组织句子。避免用“具有重大意义”“填补空白”“充分证明”代替分析。区分“报道指出”“材料显示”“本文据此提出”的证据层级；单一遗址的发现不能自动推出整个区域的社会组织或文明起源。

保留必要的专业概念，解释其在本文中的用法，不靠堆砌理论名称制造深度。历史分期、文化类型与绝对年代属于不同概念；发现、调查、发掘、地层关系不能混用。中文语序可以重写，但不得把英文中的可能、相关或局部结论改成确定、因果或普遍结论。

## Final language pass

Check quotations, names, dates, book editions, transliterations, abbreviations, references and captions together. Count English words and Chinese characters according to the brief, excluding references if specified. Length is a constraint, not a quality proxy. Preserve the author's meaning during revision and flag a substantive reinterpretation instead of silently rewriting their position.
