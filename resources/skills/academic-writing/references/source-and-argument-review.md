# Review the inference, then the prose

Use this pass before export; apply it once to the whole draft and once to the
specific defects found. More rounds are justified by a new defect, not a wish to
make the language sound increasingly academic. Save review reasoning outside the
manuscript.

## 1. Pivotal passage review

For each definition, mechanism, method or headline result that drives the thesis,
put these side by side in the ledger:

1. Exact draft sentence and source identity/edition.
2. Inspected passage with locator and its surrounding qualification.
3. Source population, setting, unit of observation and actual study design.
4. What the passage supports; what is the present writer's inference.
5. A plausible interpretation the same passage does not rule out.

Resolve words with different disciplinary meanings before combining sources.
A shared word such as recovery, culture or network does not make two constructs
interchangeable. Distinguish necessary from sufficient conditions, intention from
outcome, an author's contested definition from a field-wide consensus, a proposed
explanation from an observed motive, and correlation from intervention effects.

For theories, cite the edition actually inspected. A review's discussion of an
earlier author is a secondary interpretation, not proof that the primary text was
read. Online-first year, issue year, manuscript PDF page and printed journal page
are separate fields. Record the distinction instead of guessing a locator.

For a scanned PDF, wrapper/abstract/reference text is not the body. Check whether
the returned passages contain the relevant section. Inspect actual pages or use
an available supported OCR route, retaining its page range and provenance. If
that capability is unavailable, keep the passage unread; metadata cannot repair
this evidence gap. Never report externally supplied OCR as native extraction.

## 2. Author material and scope

For a reflective essay, compare each first-person assertion with the supplied
account. Retain concrete details and the user's stated purposes; delete invented
devices, reactions, identities, motivations and chronology. Limited recall is not
a license to invent a new psychological statement such as “I cannot remember
why.” For a conceptual essay without observations, label illustrative scenarios
as hypothetical. For a proposal, proposed activity is not a completed result.

One site, firm, campus or archive is one case. Explain why it is informative
without turning it into a population estimate. A search that failed to find a
report describes access in this run, not absence of research in the field.

## 3. A positive argument under a serious alternative

Write the strongest defensible answer in one sentence. Then test the best rival
against the same material, including ordinary or unromantic explanations. The
categories may overlap: value, compliance, coping, negotiation and opposition
need not be mutually exclusive. Select the interpretation justified by the
evidence, retain a genuine ambiguity where needed, and state what the analysis
still contributes. Do not replace the argument with repeated “cannot conclude”
sentences or silently change the task to an exercise in limitations.

Reverse-outline paragraphs by the analytical work they actually do. A paragraph
that merely restates the prior conclusion should be cut or develop a concrete
implication. Move tool errors, retrieval attempts, revision dialogue and QA
claims into the evidence/revision record unless they are the subject of the work.

## 4. End-to-end consistency

Compare introduction/thesis, section conclusions and final conclusion in one
pass. If a central classification is conditional in the body, it remains
conditional in the ending. Verify every absolute, causal, representative or
novelty claim; do not merely add a generic limitation paragraph.

For proposals, map each objective to the observable evidence needed to distinguish
alternatives, minimum materials, method limits, entry/stop condition and fallback.
Do not block an independent objective because a different objective lacks its
materials. Methods are chosen because they answer a question, not because they
sound advanced. Estimated schedules and resources stay proposed, not promised.

Freeze the manuscript only after this pass. Record genuine unresolved questions
and the actual reviewer type. A hash binds a review to bytes; it is not a substitute
for reading and judging the draft.
