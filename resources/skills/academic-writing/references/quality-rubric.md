# Anchored quality review

This is an operational synthesis of published guidance, not a validated universal grading scale. The user's actual rubric/venue governs when available. Do not infer course marks or assign a fabricated precision score. Review the final draft and source material, not the writer's promises.

Use **strong / adequate / revision_needed / unassessable** for every dimension. Strong means sustained, specific execution; adequate means the core purpose is fulfilled with localized limitations; revision_needed identifies a material defect; unassessable means required evidence is unavailable. For a dimension not applicable to a genre, explain that in the reason and judge whether the genre's appropriate alternative is satisfied.

| Record key | Ask | Strong performance |
|---|---|---|
| task | Does this fulfill the requested intellectual task? | Audience, genre, language, length and deliverable agree with the brief. |
| contribution | What does the reader learn that the setup made uncertain? | A bounded, worthwhile answer at the appropriate course/venue level. No invented novelty. |
| argument | Why does the evidence license the conclusion? | Explicit, defensible inferential bridges; concepts do analytical work. |
| evidence | Are pivotal claims traceable and adequately supported? | Inspected relevant passages, accurate attribution, appropriate material and coverage. |
| precision | Are disciplinary concepts, scales and methods used correctly? | Terms distinguish plausible interpretations; chronology/causal strength are accurate. |
| alternatives | Does the account withstand the relevant challenge? | Strong rival, conflicting evidence or design limitations actually constrain the conclusion. |
| structure | Does each part move the reasoning forward? | Necessary sequence, developed paragraphs and an earned ending without redundant loops. |
| language | Can the reader follow precise reasoning without decoding ornament? | Clear, economical target-language prose, consistent terms, appropriate voice. |
| transparency | Can the reader tell what was borrowed, observed, inferred and done? | Access/method limits, provenance and applicable disclosures are truthful. |

Record a draft passage or location, a reason and an actionable correction for each verdict. Citation identity, claim support, reasoning, style and rendered layout are separate checks. A brilliant paragraph cannot compensate for invented evidence. Unsupported pivotal claims, invented methods or wrong-task output prevent a final-quality claim regardless of other strengths.

## Self-review and comparative evaluation

If no independent reviewer is available, label self-review explicitly. For comparisons, freeze the task and source packet, use the same model/length/tools budget, anonymize drafts, swap presentation order and retain disagreements. Judge source support against the actual passages. Record concrete strengths and defects before an overall preference. Do not reward extra length, decorative headings or a model's confident QA label.

The three supplied user topics are development cases, not gold answers or held-out tests. Unseen disciplines and constrained-evidence tasks test transfer. Human subject-competent review is needed to establish broad scholarly quality; a small same-model pilot only guides iteration. “Industry-leading” additionally requires a defined competitor set, representative independent cases, blinded human judgments and reported uncertainty.

## Guidance underlying this rubric

- AAC&U [Written Communication VALUE rubric](https://www.ubalt.edu/_migrated-binaries/ubalt.edu/institutional_effectiveness/uploads/documents/assessment_handbook/written%20communication.pdf): adapt to context; not a direct grading instrument.
- Harvard [Thesis](https://writingcenter.fas.harvard.edu/thesis) and [Counterargument](https://writingcenter.fas.harvard.edu/counterargument): focused contestable claims and substantive objections.
- UW–Madison [Reverse outline](https://dept.writing.wisc.edu/wac/using-a-reverse-outline-to-revise/): inspect actual organization.
- Fudan [学术写作的要点与步骤](https://news.fudan.edu.cn/2019/0606/c1015a97003/page.htm): claims, reasons and evidence.
- AHA [Professional standards](https://www.historians.org/resource/statement-on-standards-of-professional-conduct/): faithful, retraceable use of sources.
- [EQUATOR](https://www.equator-network.org/about-us/what-is-a-reporting-guideline/) / [STROBE](https://www.strobe-statement.org/): reporting completeness is distinct from validity.
- [ALCE](https://aclanthology.org/2023.emnlp-main.398/) and [MT-Bench evaluation study](https://arxiv.org/abs/2306.05685): citation support and judge limitations. Their task results are not evidence that this Skill outperforms competitors.
