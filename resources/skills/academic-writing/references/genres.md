# Choose the intellectual task

The user's assignment or actual venue instructions take precedence. If absent, state a modest genre assumption and proceed; do not invent a rubric. A short length does not identify a genre.

| Genre | Reader needs | Appropriate structure and evidence |
|---|---|---|
| Critical / theoretical essay | A focused, contestable interpretation and why it is preferable | Analytical problem → argument developed through concrete material → serious rival or qualification → earned conclusion. Read theory at the passage/edition level. No compulsory abstract, methods, figures or novelty claim. |
| Reflective academic essay | A situated experience reconsidered through concepts | User-supplied event/context → initial interpretation → conceptual analysis and alternatives → revised understanding/implication. First person is appropriate. No fabricated autobiography; request missing experiences or provide a clearly hypothetical example instead. |
| Historical / archaeological commentary | What records establish and what interpretation adds | Identify source type, provenance, chronology, spatial scale and uncertainties. Separate observation, classification and inference. News of a discovery is not a stratigraphic excavation report. A commentary may be valuable without claiming new fieldwork. |
| Narrative literature synthesis | An answer organized by findings, disagreements or approaches | Use a transparent bounded search and source selection, critical synthesis and unresolved issues. Do not call it systematic merely because searches or a table exist. |
| Systematic / scoping review | Reproducible selection and design-appropriate synthesis | Route evidence collection to literature-deep-review and the appropriate reporting guideline; quantitative pooling to evidence-synthesis-meta-analysis when justified. Writing does not supply missing screening or risk-of-bias work. |
| Empirical manuscript | What was actually done, found and warranted | Match design and venue; preserve frozen data/results. Methods, results and uncertainty must reflect real work. Use applicable reporting standards (e.g. JARS, CONSORT, STROBE, PRISMA), not a generic checklist as a validity score. |
| Mathematical / technical argument | Definitions, assumptions and a checkable derivation or construction | Match disciplinary conventions; proofs need valid steps, not an artificial opposing-view paragraph. Distinguish established results, conjectures, numerical examples and proved claims. |
| Proposal | A feasible planned study, not completed findings | Route to research-proposal-writing. Keep planned methods separate from obtained results. |

## Depth and contribution

For coursework, a defensible original reading of assigned material may fulfill the task. For a journal article, locate a specific contribution relative to an inspected scholarly conversation and an actual venue. Originality can be interpretive, synthetic, methodological or empirical. It does not always mean new numerical data. Neither genre licenses a fabricated literature gap.

For source-poor requests, narrow the scope or deliver a visibly provisional conceptual outline. A shortage of observations cannot be solved by importing Methods/Results headings. Preserve complexity without turning every sentence into a hedge; state what is established directly and qualify only the inference that exceeds it.

## Export handoff

Give the document skill the frozen brief and manuscript. Critical essays may need only a title, developed prose, useful section headings and references. Do not inherit 2500-word/4000-character floors or DOI-link requirements when they contradict the brief. Classical books and archival records can be central sources without DOIs. Keep short comments short; avoid decorative diagrams and executive summaries unless the audience needs them.
