# Source use and inferential integrity

Track three separate questions: does the source exist with this identity/edition, was the relevant passage inspected, and does that passage support this use? A resolving DOI answers only part of the first question. Abstracts and snippets cannot stand in for unseen methods, page numbers or full-text quotations.

## Bounded acquisition

Start from supplied material. Use discovery tools to fill named gaps, checking foundational texts, relevant current debate and contrary interpretations. Select databases by discipline/language; English journal indices alone do not cover Chinese scholarship, archival records or classical books. Prefer an original text for what its author argued, and an empirical report for what its authors observed. Secondary analysis can be appropriate if identified honestly. Save query/date/source and unsuccessful searches without claiming exhaustive absence.

For a short essay, define the few indispensable source functions before searching. After one expansion pass, decide whether to draft, narrow the claim, or identify a concrete missing source. Do not retrieve dozens of adjacent theories to satisfy an arbitrary citation quota. For a full review, expand the plan to match its declared coverage instead of applying the short-essay limit.

## Inspect and record

Tool success is not reading success. A PDF returned by ordinary webfetch as `%PDF-`, `obj`, compressed streams or replacement-character garbage has not yielded readable evidence. Stop that route, mark the source unread, and use the supported PDF acquisition/reading path described in [runtime-and-delivery.md](runtime-and-delivery.md). Do not repeatedly fetch the same binary as text, count it as full text, or cite its unseen content. An HTML error/login page is not the requested article either.

- Verify author, title, year and edition from authoritative records. Record book translator/publisher/edition and locator where relevant. Cite the edition actually consulted; do not silently combine its date with another edition's page numbering.
- Preserve accessible source text with a stable URL or identifier, retrieval date and scope of access. Keep copyright-limited excerpts and locators sufficient for audit; do not redistribute full copyrighted books in the deliverable.
- For each consequential borrowed claim, record the passage, its context, what it supports, and any qualification. Split a compound claim when support differs.
- Distinguish facts reported by a source, the source author's interpretation, the current writer's inference, hypothetical illustration and user-provided experience. Mark inference with its warrant; it need not be disguised as someone else's finding.
- Record contradictory material and explain the final treatment. “Context” or “mentions” is not support. Do not discard adverse sources simply because they weaken the thesis.

## Different source types

A media account may establish how an event or phenomenon was represented; it does not establish population prevalence. Several outlets repeating one press release are not independent observations. A primary text is primary relative to the question, not intrinsically infallible. A historical object's provenance and dating uncertainty matter before drawing social conclusions. A clinical result requires its population/design/uncertainty; a philosophical claim requires accurate interpretation and argument, not a cohort count.

A source visible only as metadata stays a candidate. An abstract can support a narrowly attributed abstract-level statement; flag this access boundary. For an exact quotation, inspect the exact text and locator. Verify translations, negation and surrounding qualifications. Never invent page numbers or use a quoted secondary passage as if the primary source was inspected.

## Integrity and author responsibility

No invented experiments, interviews, archives, personal experiences, data, quotations or references. Missing material remains missing. Respect the applicable assignment or journal policy for assistance and disclosure; do not invent author contributions or ethics approvals. Report actual assistance and limits. A structurally complete document does not make unperformed research publishable.
