# One brief, explicit source rules

At the first substantial writing step, save the fields in
[writing-brief.json](../assets/writing-brief.json). Fill them from the request and
supplied material; mark unspecified fields as unspecified. A format preference is
not an intellectual thesis. A proposed word count or source limit chosen by the
agent is an agent assumption, never a user requirement.

Keep three separate language decisions: conversation language, manuscript
language, and permitted publication languages. An English manuscript can cite
Chinese scholarship unless the task excludes it. Follow the selected citation
style for original titles, transliteration and bracketed translations; translating
a title never changes the publication's language. Title Case applies to English
headings when requested or chosen by the venue; bibliography titles follow their
citation style, and Chinese headings have no capitalization rule.

## Source policy

Empty `allowed_languages` and `allowed_types` mean no task restriction. Set
`forbid_cjk` only when explicitly requested for this deliverable. Do not infer it
from `language: en`. Use source type and evidential role separately:

| Source | Suitable uses to consider | What it does not establish by itself |
|---|---|---|
| Scholarly article/book | Its inspected theory, analysis, reported findings | Correctness of every claim, universal applicability |
| Official report/data/record | The documented observation or administrative record | Independent verification, causal explanation |
| Newspaper/media/blog | Attributed reporting, discourse or representation being analyzed | Population prevalence, a peer-reviewed result, a full excavation report |
| User account | That person's supplied recollection or perspective | New observation, unreported motive or other people's reactions |

Publication venue is not a proxy for what a passage supports. Verify source
identity, language, type and the relevant passage. Record a shared provenance
group when several publications repeat the same announcement or dataset. Their
agreement is not independent corroboration.

Plan coverage by **function**: the central construct or method; concrete material;
the strongest rival; and relevant scope/context. Inspect foundational authority
when an argument turns on its definition. Fill a missing function, not a citation
quota. Four sources may be enough for one narrow exercise and inadequate for a
field review. Neither four nor ten is a universal quality threshold. If access
fails, use an authorized alternate, reduce the dependent claim, or identify a
specific blocker. Do not quietly lower a promised research scope.

On a new task constraint, update this brief, identify affected claims and rewrite
them before changing the bibliography. Preserve the superseded version. After
compaction, read the brief and current manuscript before research resumes. If a
steer names an unrelated topic while the saved brief and attached materials name
another, clarify the intended switch before overwriting the current work.
