## Binding requirements

- English-language scholarly publications only. No news, media, blogs, marketing
  articles, or translated titles disguising originally Chinese publications.
  Verify the actual publication language and scholarly status, not only a
  discovery index's title or language field.
- Rewrite claims when their sources are excluded; removing bibliography entries
  alone would leave unsupported content.
- Distinguish direct Banwei evidence from recovery, involution, lying-flat,
  resistance and commodification research. A limited unsuccessful search is not
  proof that no English Banwei research exists.
- English manuscript and bibliography, using romanized terms where needed.
  Main title and section headings use Title Case. Bibliographic titles follow
  the selected citation style and verified publication metadata.
- Preserve substantive analysis, a clear contribution, and a serious alternative
  interpretation. Do not replace the essay with a list of caveats, a sequence of
  article summaries or a claim of empirical findings without data.
- Inspect source passages supporting consequential claims. Metadata and abstract
  access must remain visibly limited in the evidence record.
- Final DOCX and PDF use one frozen manuscript; include a real References heading,
  rendered italics rather than Markdown markers, consistent academic typography,
  readable URLs, hanging references and page numbers.
- Run actual document validators; inspect every final rendered page. Preserve
  distinction between client production, client QA and external verification.
