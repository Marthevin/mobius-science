remain in a local private snapshot outside Git. Counts from that snapshot:

Turns include initial instructions, planned stage advances, preference changes,
corrections, and one operator prompt sent to the wrong topic. Do not divide these
numbers into a “failure rate” or count every stage advance as a correction.

External corrective prompts and any tool errors in the next runs must be logged.
