The first render revealed two real layout faults that the structural checks did not catch: CJK characters appeared as squares in LibreOffice, and a figure was clipped by inherited exact line spacing. The template and QA path now bundle and expose the open-licensed CJK font, remove conflicting built-in Word theme mappings, give picture paragraphs natural height, and check font embedding in the proof. A built-in Title border was also removed. Final pages were rendered again after these fixes.

Writing review: the literature example attributes distinct claims to the three checked papers and limits inference across archaeology and linguistics; it explicitly disclaims systematic coverage. The data report and appendix derive counts, means, sample standard deviations and the scatter plot from the same 150 input rows; they do not claim population inference, causality, classification performance or a new biological finding. UCI's recommended dataset citation replaced an initially incorrect repository-year citation during review. The dataset record and publisher links are listed in [`data/SOURCE.md`](data/SOURCE.md).

These are **Skill workflow regression examples**, not journal submissions. They validate layout and evidence-boundary behavior in the direct generator→DOCX→LibreOffice proof path. This record does not establish that every future LLM-authored report is publication-ready, or that Microsoft Word paginates identically. The target journal's template and a final Word inspection remain necessary for a submission. A live DeepSeek/OpenCode/client generation turn was not part of this deterministic test.
